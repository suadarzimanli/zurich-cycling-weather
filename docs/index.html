Zurich: cycling and weather

Has cycling in Zurich become less sensitive to rain? Fifteen years of open counting data, 2011 to 2026.

Forecasting tomorrow's bike traffic

Each evening, the models forecast the next day's bike count at 19 city counters. They were trained on data up to May 2025 and tested from 1 June 2025 to 22 November 2026. The simplest forecast is "the same weekday last week". The graph neural network (Graph WaveNet) also uses the network of nearby counters.

What we found

  • Rain matters a little less than it used to. At the 9 city counters observed in both periods, a wet day cut daytime cycling by 33% in 2011–2014 and by 29% in 2022–2026. Across all 33 city counters, the rain effect weakens by about 4 percentage points per decade.
  • The change is specific to cycling. Over the same years, walking did not become less sensitive to rain.
  • E-bikes do not look like the main reason. Cold and heat effects did not shrink more than the rain effect, and the change is not larger in the suburbs, where e-bikes help most.
  • Rainy days do not bring more cars. Car traffic is about 1.5% lower on wet days, and this has not changed since 2012.
  • Rain still matters a lot. Cycling in Zurich is becoming somewhat more robust to rain, but it is far from weather-proof.

How it was done

Daily counts between 6:00 and 20:00 are modelled with a Poisson regression. Each counter is compared with itself within the same month, with separate levels for each weekday and for holidays, so the weather effect comes from day-to-day changes in weather. Standard errors allow for correlation within each week. The plan, the predictions and the exact tests were locked with checksums before the counts were joined with the weather. Several checks were run: a second weather station, no data cleaning, and the replacement of counting devices.

Data

  • Bicycle and pedestrian counts: City of Zurich, Tiefbauamt (CC0)
  • Car counts: City of Zurich, Dienstabteilung Verkehr (CC0)
  • Canton bike counts: Tiefbauamt Kanton Zürich (open government data)
  • Weather: MeteoSwiss, ten-minute values (source: MeteoSwiss)
  • School holidays: City of Zurich, Schulamt (CC0)
